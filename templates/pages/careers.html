Join Our Team

At Gracious Living, LLC, our caregivers are an important part of helping individuals remain safe, comfortable, and supported in their homes.


We are looking for dependable, compassionate, and professional individuals who share our commitment to providing respectful, high-quality nonmedical care.


We welcome applicants interested in:

Caregiver positions
Homemaker and Companion positions
Personal Care Assistance
Respite Care
Overnight and Live-In assignments
Per Diem opportunities


What We Look For 

Applicants should be compassionate, dependable, professional, respectful of client privacy and boundaries, and able to work independently in clients' homes.


Certain assignments may require reliable transportation, a valid driver's license, and current automobile insurance.


Background Screening 

Connecticut law requires Gracious Living, LLC to require prospective employees who may provide homemaker or companion services to submit to a comprehensive background check before an offer of employment is extended. 


Applicants who advance in the hiring process will receive the required background-check disclosures, authorization forms, criminal-conviction and professional-discipline statement, and other applicable pre-employment documentation.
