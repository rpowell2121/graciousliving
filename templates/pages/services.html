Frequently Asked Questions

Please reach us at info@graciouslivingllc.com if you cannot find an answer to your question.

How quickly can services begin?

Depending on your loved one's needs, services can often begin within 24 to 72 hours after the initial consultation and completion of the care plan. We strive to respond promptly to every referral and urgent request.

Is there a minimum number of hours?

Yes. To ensure continuity of care, 2-hour visits require a commitment of at least 3 visits per week, and 3-hour or longer visits require at least 2 visits per week. We're happy to walk through scheduling options during your complimentary consultation.

Do you accept Medicaid?

Yes. Gracious Living is an approved Connecticut Home Care Program (CHCPE) provider, and we accept HUSKY C, Connecticut's Medicaid program for adults 65 and older and individuals who are blind or disabled. If your loved one is enrolled in HUSKY C and has been assessed as eligible for the CHCPE Medicaid-waiver track, we can provide services under that program, in addition to private pay and private insurance. Eligibility depends on income, assets, and level of care needed, so we're happy to walk through your specific situation during your complimentary consultation.

Can I change my care schedule?

Absolutely. We understand that care needs evolve over time. Whether you need additional hours, fewer visits, or changes to your schedule, we'll work with you to update your personalized care plan with advance notice.

Can I meet the caregiver before services begin?

Whenever possible, we strive to match clients with caregivers who best fit their needs and personality. We carefully select compassionate, qualified caregivers and are committed to creating a comfortable and trusting relationship from the very beginning.

What areas do you serve?

We proudly serve families throughout New Haven County, Fairfield County, and Litchfield County , providing dependable, compassionate non-medical in-home care.
