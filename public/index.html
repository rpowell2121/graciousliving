Frequently Asked Questions

Please reach us at info@graciouslivingllc.com if you cannot find an answer to your question.

What areas do you serve?

Gracious Living LLC proudly serves clients throughout New Haven County, Fairfield County, and Litchfield County. If you're unsure whether we serve your community, please contact us. We're happy to discuss your needs.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept a variety of payment options, including Self-Pay (Private Pay), long-term care insurance from participating plans, and the Connecticut Home Care Program for eligible clients. If your insurance provider is not currently accepted, we'd love to hear from you. We are continually evaluating new insurance partnerships and are happy to review credentialing opportunities with additional carriers whenever possible.

What is your minimum service requirement?

Minimum Hours of Service

  • Private Pay: Minimum 4-hour visit
  • Long-Term Care Insurance: Minimum 2-hour visit (unless otherwise authorized)
  • Medicaid & Government Programs: Visits are scheduled according to the authorized care plan and payer requirements.
  • Veterans Benefits: Visit schedules follow the approved authorization.

Questions about scheduling? Contact us for a complimentary care consultation.

Why does Gracious Living have a four-hour minimum for private-pay services?

Our four-hour minimum allows caregivers to provide comprehensive, unhurried care while minimizing frequent caregiver changes and travel time. This approach promotes continuity of care, stronger caregiver-client relationships, and a higher quality experience for our clients and their families.

As Gracious Living continues to grow, this minimum service requirement may be reviewed and revised based on client demand, staffing availability, and operational needs.

Do you offer live-in care?

Yes. Gracious Living LLC offers live-in care services for clients who require around-the-clock assistance while remaining in the comfort and familiarity of their own home. We work with each family to develop a personalized care plan based on their unique needs.

What services does Gracious Living LLC provide?

Our caregivers provide compassionate, non-medical home care services, including Personal Care, Companion Care, Homemaker Services, Transportation Assistance, Respite Care, and Live-In Care. Visit our Services page for a full breakdown of each category.

Are your caregivers trained to care for individuals with Alzheimer's disease and other forms of dementia?

Yes. Our caregivers receive specialized Alzheimer's and dementia care training that prepares them to support individuals experiencing memory loss with patience, compassion, and respect. Our approach focuses on maintaining dignity, encouraging independence whenever possible, establishing familiar routines, reducing anxiety and confusion, and partnering closely with families throughout the caregiving journey.

How do I get started?

Getting started is easy: contact Gracious Living LLC, schedule your complimentary consultation, meet with our team to discuss your needs, receive a personalized care plan, and begin services with a carefully matched caregiver. We're here to guide you every step of the way.
